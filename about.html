He started in 2008 as a QA tester at Indemaj, then moved through front-end, back-end and architecture in the same building. Four years at e-commerce scale followed, first as a PHP developer at Souq.com, then as Lead Developer at MarkaVIP, where he translated stakeholder requirements into technical specifications and directed a development team through delivery. By 2015 he was Technical Team Leader at ShopGo, accountable for delivery standards and client satisfaction as much as for code.

The second track ran the entire time. He was teaching from 2007 — first computing at MLC, including off-site programmes for Arab Potash, the UNHCR refugee programme, USAID and the Prime Ministry — then building the web development curriculum at Pioneers Academy across five years. In 2018 he became Lead Trainer at Al Hussein Technical University, coaching over 500 students through full-stack tracks in a national programme aimed at youth employability. 86% of those graduates improved their income.

Since 2020 the two tracks have been one job. As Coding Academy Manager at Orange Jordan he runs two locations — the Amman hub and Aqaba, which serves the south of the country and sits inside Jordan's smart-city development vision. Alongside it he co-founded Upskills Training & Consultations in 2017, where he advises on digital transformation, Agile and Scrum adoption, curriculum design and technical delivery.
