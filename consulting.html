01 — Services

Twelve ways to work together.

Grouped into three practices. Every line below is backed by a role in the record, not a capability statement.

Consulting practices

Digital transformation advisory

Setting the direction and the sequence — where technology actually changes how the organisation works, and what has to be true before it can. Grounded in IT governance and strategic planning from a 2008 IT department through to a national telecom's academy strategy.

Technical & architecture advisory

System design, technical specification, performance and security, reviewed by someone who wrote the code rather than only the slides. Eight years in production at Indemaj, Souq.com, MarkaVIP and ShopGo.

Web & CMS development

Full-stack delivery across PHP, MySQL, Linux and Apache, plus CMS builds run end to end for customers through Upskills — project goals, priorities and delivery monitored against them.

E-commerce & platform engineering

Magento and large-catalogue e-commerce at real scale: back-end components, third-party integration, data storage, and the complex performance and architectural problems that come with it.

Coding academy design & operations

The signature offer — built and run three academies across two cities. Curriculum, cohort model, trainer hiring, KPI design and the operations that hold it together. Very few people can say this.

Curriculum development & accreditation

Built the web development curriculum at Pioneers and the full-stack tracks at HTU, and contributed to a nationally accredited certificate programme. Knows what an accreditor actually asks for.

Corporate & institutional training

On-site technical training delivered to Arab Potash, the UNHCR refugee programme, USAID and the Jordanian Prime Ministry, alongside university workshops and seminars.

ICT workforce development

National-scale programme design aimed at employability and economic participation. The HTU brief was exactly this: 500+ developers trained, 86% of graduates improving their income.

Delivery governance & PMO setup

Project planning, software project management and project coordination — the three strongest signals on his professional record, and the discipline behind every delivery below.

Agile & Scrum adoption

Getting cadence into teams that have the talent but not the rhythm. Practised across engineering floors and academy operations alike, and claimed as a consulting line on his own profile.

Technical team leadership

Directing developers through design, development, testing and debugging; owning code review culture; monitoring and evaluating staff progress. Lead Developer at MarkaVIP, Team Leader at ShopGo.

Performance management & KPIs

Setting objectives and KPIs, reviewing them with the team, and adapting the plan when they slip — plus regular 1:1s, appraisal and stakeholder reporting against organisational benchmarks.
